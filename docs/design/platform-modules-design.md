## Problem Statement

Growing an audience across social platforms (X, LinkedIn, Gmail) requires consistent, high-quality engagement — but the effort is fragmented across apps, the timing is critical, and most people can't sustain it manually. The real growth lever isn't responding to what comes to you (reactive) — it's **finding and joining the right conversations proactively** in your niche.

Current tools either aggregate notifications (reactive only) or automate posting (not engagement). Nothing helps you **show up in the right conversations, in your voice, at the right time, across platforms** — which is how audiences actually grow.

## Demand Evidence

- Founder is primary user and built pingi to solve their own audience growth problem on X
- Real pain: manually checking multiple platforms led to missed opportunities and inconsistent engagement
- The problem is cumulative — not one dramatic failure, but the constant drain of context-switching and missed windows
- No external users yet — pre-product stage, conviction bet based on personal experience

## Status Quo

Current workaround for most creators/founders:
- Check X, LinkedIn, Gmail separately multiple times per day
- Mentally prioritize which replies/mentions matter
- Write each response from scratch, matching platform tone
- Miss time-sensitive conversations (especially on X where speed matters)
- Never proactively find conversations to join — only respond to what arrives
- Some use scheduling tools (Buffer, Hootsuite) for posting, but nothing for engagement
- The "do nothing" competitor is strong: most people just accept they'll miss things

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Target user:** Startup founders and creators who are building in public, active on X and LinkedIn, and know that engagement drives growth — but can't sustain the effort manually. They're not social media managers; they're builders who need social presence as a growth channel.

**Narrowest wedge:** X proactive engagement module via Telegram. Pingi scans trending conversations in the user's niche, drafts engagement-ready replies, and pushes them to Telegram for one-tap action. Prove that this measurably grows X followers before expanding to other platforms.

## Constraints

- Solo founder, pre-revenue
- Substantial codebase already built (Telegram bot, web dashboard, 3 platform connectors, AI drafting, Stripe billing, invite codes, x-engage bot)
- Platform API limitations (LinkedIn API is restrictive; Twitter API has rate limits and cost)
- "Proactive engagement suggestions" quality is critical — bad suggestions = immediate churn
- Must feel like YOUR voice, not generic AI — voice profiles are essential
- Different platforms require genuinely different engagement strategies

## Premises

1. **Pingi is an audience growth engine, not an inbox manager.** Reactive reply handling is a feature; proactive engagement is the product.
2. **Different platforms need different engagement strategies.** X rewards speed, wit, and trending-thread participation. LinkedIn rewards thoughtful commentary and relationship building. One-size-fits-all won't work.
3. **Telegram push interface is the right primary UX** — meets users where they are, enables ambient one-tap engagement throughout the day.
4. **Proactive engagement is the key growth lever** — finding the right conversations to join, not just responding faster to the ones that come to you.
5. **The product scope is wide for zero users** — architectural focus on modules allows launching one platform first while preserving the multi-platform vision.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: "Proactive Engagement Engine" — Unified Multi-Platform

One product with proactive engagement as the core loop across all platforms simultaneously. Multi-platform from day 1.

- **Effort:** M
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Pros:** Matches full vision; multi-platform differentiator; reuses x-engage infra
- **Cons:** "Find the right conversations" is hard to get right; quality of suggestions is critical; large surface area to maintain

### Approach B: "Platform Modules" — Shared Core, Pluggable Platforms ✅ CHOSEN

Shared AI engine + Telegram interface, but each platform is a distinct module with its own engagement strategy. Users pick which platforms to activate. X module focuses on speed + trending. LinkedIn module focuses on relationship + authority.

- **Effort:** M
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Pros:** Best of both worlds; users self-select platforms; can launch X first; cleaner architecture
- **Cons:** More engineering complexity upfront; modules could feel disconnected

### Approach C: "X Growth First, Expand Later"

Launch X-only with proactive engagement. Prove measurable follower growth, then add LinkedIn.

- **Effort:** S-M
- **Risk:** Low
- **Pros:** Fastest to prove thesis; X growth is most measurable; lower support burden
- **Cons:** Delays multi-platform vision; some users won't try without LinkedIn

## Recommended Approach

**Approach B architecture with Approach C launch sequencing** — build the module architecture so multi-platform is structurally supported, but launch X-only first to prove the proactive engagement thesis. LinkedIn module starts only after X module shows traction. This gives you the right architecture without the risk of launching everything at once.

### Core Shared Layer

Core functionality exists but is not yet structured as a shared core; the refactor extracts and formalizes it into a clean interface that modules consume.

- **AI Engine:** Claude-powered drafter with voice profiles (functionality exists, needs extraction into shared service)
- **Telegram Bot Interface:** Push notifications with Send/Edit/Skip (functionality exists, needs module-aware routing)
- **Urgency Scoring:** Time-based prioritization (functionality exists)
- **User Management:** Auth, billing, onboarding (functionality exists via Supabase + Stripe)
- **Error Handling & Degradation:** If a platform API is down or rate-limited, the module falls back to reactive-only mode. User is notified via Telegram: "X proactive scanning paused — rate limit, will resume in {time}." Other modules continue unaffected.

### Platform Module Interface

Each module implements a standard contract:

```typescript
interface PlatformModule {
  // Identity
  id: string;               // e.g. "x", "linkedin"
  displayName: string;

  // Reactive: fetch inbound engagement items
  fetchItems(userId: string, since: Date): Promise<EngagementItem[]>;

  // Proactive: scan for high-value conversations to join
  scanOpportunities(userId: string, niche: NicheProfile): Promise<Opportunity[]>;

  // Publish: send the approved reply/engagement
  publish(userId: string, item: EngagementItem, draft: string): Promise<PublishResult>;

  // Strategy: platform-specific rules for drafting
  getStrategyConfig(): PlatformStrategy;

  // Metrics: fetch growth data for this platform
  getMetrics(userId: string, period: DateRange): Promise<PlatformMetrics>;
}
```

The Telegram bot calls `fetchItems()` and `scanOpportunities()` on each enabled module, merges results into the drip queue, and dispatches to the correct module's `publish()` when the user taps Send.

### Proactive Suggestion Quality Signals

A conversation is "high-value" if it scores well on these signals:
1. **Topic relevance:** Thread topic matches user's niche keywords (weighted by TF-IDF against niche profile)
2. **Thread velocity:** Thread is gaining engagement quickly (>5 replies in last hour) — signals visibility
3. **Author authority:** Thread author has >1K followers in the user's niche — replies get seen
4. **Recency:** Thread is <4 hours old — early replies get more visibility
5. **Gap detection:** No expert in the user's niche has replied yet — opportunity to be the authoritative voice

Default cadence: scan every 30 minutes, push max 5 proactive suggestions per day. Tunable per user.

### Key Type Definitions

```typescript
interface NicheProfile {
  topics: string[];           // e.g. ["AI agents", "developer tools", "startup growth"]
  keywords: string[];         // extracted from user's recent posts + manual input
  excludeTopics?: string[];   // topics to ignore
}

interface Opportunity {
  platform: string;
  threadId: string;
  threadUrl: string;
  threadAuthor: { handle: string; followerCount: number };
  threadSnippet: string;      // first 280 chars of the thread
  relevanceScore: number;     // 0-100 based on quality signals
  suggestedDraft: string;     // AI-generated reply
  foundAt: Date;
}

// EngagementItem — already defined in src/types.ts (inbound mentions/replies)
// PlatformStrategy — { maxLength, tone, timing, frequencyRules }
// PlatformMetrics — { followerDelta, impressions, engagementRate, period }
```

### Publish Failure Handling

When a user taps "Send" and `publish()` fails:
1. **Retry once** after 3 seconds (handles transient network errors)
2. If retry fails, notify user via Telegram: "Couldn't send — tap to retry" with a Retry button
3. **Idempotency:** Each publish attempt includes a unique `idempotency_key` to prevent double-posting on retry
4. **Auth expiry:** If the error is an auth token issue, prompt user to re-connect their account

### Voice Profile Lifecycle

- **Minimum viable input:** 3 example posts/replies (onboarding won't complete with fewer)
- **Generation:** Claude analyzes examples to extract tone descriptors (formality, humor, verbosity, emoji usage) and stores them as structured voice config in `voice_profiles` table
- **Refresh:** User can update examples anytime; voice profile regenerates on save
- **Cold start:** If user provides only 3 examples, drafts may feel generic — pingi improves as it learns from Edit feedback (user corrections refine the profile over time)

### Data & Privacy

- **Stored data:** OAuth tokens (encrypted at rest in Supabase), fetched engagement items (retained 30 days), voice profiles, engagement metrics
- **Account deletion:** All user data including OAuth tokens, items, voice profiles, and metrics deleted within 48 hours of request
- **No data sharing:** User content is never shared across accounts or used for model training
- **OAuth scopes:** Request minimum necessary scopes (read + write for X, read-only for LinkedIn until publish is supported)

### Twitter API Cost Model

Twitter API v2 tiers and proactive scanning budget:
- **One `scanOpportunities()` call** ≈ 3-5 API requests (search recent tweets by niche keywords + fetch thread context)
- **Per user per day:** 48 scans × 4 requests avg = ~192 requests/day
- **10 beta users:** ~1,920 requests/day = ~57,600/month
- **Basic tier ($100/month):** 10,000 tweet reads/month — **not sufficient**
- **Pro tier ($5,000/month):** 1M tweet reads/month — overkill and too expensive pre-revenue

**Mitigation: Shared niche-level scans.** Instead of scanning per-user, scan per-niche. Users with overlapping niches share scan results. For 10 beta users with ~5 unique niches, this reduces to ~960 requests/day = ~28,800/month. Still exceeds Basic tier.

**Recommended approach:** Start with 2-hour scan intervals (not 30 min) and 3 suggestions/day during beta. This yields ~7,200 requests/month for 10 users with shared scans — within Basic tier. Increase frequency only after validating the feature drives growth and revenue justifies Pro tier.

### Onboarding Flow (X Module)

1. Connect X account via OAuth
2. Select 3-5 niche topics (suggested from user's recent tweets + manual input)
3. Provide 5-10 example tweets/replies for voice training
4. Link Telegram bot
5. First proactive scan runs immediately — user sees their first suggestion within 5 minutes

### X Module (Launch First)
- Reactive: Mention monitoring (exists)
- Proactive: Trending topic scanner + niche conversation finder (partially built in x-engage)
- Strategy: Speed-first, wit-rewarded, thread-jumping, optimal timing coaching
- Metrics: Follower count delta, impression trends, engagement rate

### LinkedIn Module (Launch Second)
- Reactive: Comment monitoring on posts (exists)
- Proactive: Find high-engagement posts in user's industry → suggest thoughtful comments
- Strategy: Relationship-first, authority-building, professional tone, less time-pressure
- Metrics: Connection growth, post impressions, comment engagement

### Gmail Module (Future Consideration)

Gmail is not a growth platform. The existing Gmail connector can remain as-is but is out of scope for this design. It may be revisited as a utility feature once the growth thesis is proven on X and LinkedIn.

### Architectural Refactor Needed
The current codebase has platform logic scattered across `bot/src/connectors/`, `bot/src/x-engage/`, and `src/connectors/`. The refactor would:
1. Define a `PlatformModule` interface that each platform implements
2. Move platform-specific logic into self-contained module directories
3. Keep the Telegram bot and AI drafter as shared infrastructure
4. Allow users to enable/disable modules independently
5. Each module owns its own proactive scanning logic

### Launch Plan
1. **Week 1-3:** Minimum viable refactor — extract X module into new architecture. Define `PlatformModule` interface. Consolidate existing x-engage + Twitter connector code into a single X module directory. Leave LinkedIn/Gmail connectors untouched for now; they don't need to implement the new interface until their module launches.
2. **Week 4-5:** Build proactive engagement for X module — expand trending scanner to suggest specific conversations using the quality signals above (topic relevance, thread velocity, author authority, recency, gap detection). Target: 5 high-quality suggestions per day.
3. **Week 6-7:** Get 5-10 beta users on X module via invite codes. Onboard them through the X flow. Measure: action rate on suggestions, follower growth vs. 2-week pre-pingi baseline (self-reported or API-tracked), retention after week 2.
4. **Week 8-10:** Iterate based on feedback. If X module shows traction (>50% action rate, retention >2 weeks), begin LinkedIn module using the same `PlatformModule` interface.

### LinkedIn API Feasibility Note

LinkedIn's API is restrictive — third-party apps generally cannot post comments on arbitrary posts on behalf of users. Fallback strategy: pingi surfaces "conversations to join" with drafted replies, but the user copies/pastes into LinkedIn manually. This is less magical but still valuable (the AI found the conversation + wrote the reply). If LinkedIn opens API access or a reliable browser-extension approach becomes viable, upgrade to one-tap posting.

## Open Questions

1. **Proactive suggestion quality validation:** Five quality signals are defined (topic relevance, thread velocity, author authority, recency, gap detection). Are these sufficient, or do we need additional signals like sentiment analysis or user engagement history?
2. **Platform API costs:** Twitter API v2 has pricing tiers. How much does proactive scanning cost at scale?
3. **Measurement:** How do you attribute follower growth to pingi specifically vs. organic growth? Need a clear "before/after" signal.
4. **Competition:** Tools like Typefully, Hypefury, and Shield exist for X and LinkedIn growth. How does pingi's proactive engagement angle differentiate from their scheduling + analytics features?
5. **Voice quality at scale:** Voice profiles work for one user. How well does the AI maintain distinct voices across 10, 100, 1000 users?

## Success Criteria

- 5-10 beta users actively using X module within 6 weeks
- At least 3 beta users show measurable follower growth (>10% over their 2-week pre-pingi baseline) within first month of use. Note: small sample size — supplement with qualitative signals (user self-report: "I'm getting more engagement")
- >50% of pushed proactive suggestions get acted on (Send or Edit, not Skip)
- Beta users retain for >2 weeks (don't churn after novelty wears off)
- At least 1 user voluntarily tells someone else about pingi

## Dependencies

- Twitter API access and rate limits for proactive scanning
- LinkedIn API approval for proactive engagement features
- Claude API costs at scale (proactive = more drafts = more API calls)
- Beta user recruitment — who are the first 5-10 people?
